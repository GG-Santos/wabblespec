# Required Clauses

Minimum required content per document type. Any missing required clause blocks document generation.

## Privacy Policy (GDPR)

Required inputs before generation:
- Product name
- Contact email (privacy contact)
- Data collected (list of personal data categories)
- Legal basis for processing
- Retention periods per category (if unknown: `[REVIEW REQUIRED]`)
- Third-party processors (if none: state explicitly)

Required clauses in output:
1. Who we are (data controller identity)
2. What data we collect (categories and sources)
3. Why we process data (legal basis per purpose)
4. How long we keep data (retention periods)
5. Who we share data with (third-party processors)
6. International transfers (if applicable)
7. Your rights (access, deletion, portability, objection, restriction, complaint to supervisory authority)
8. Cookies (if applicable)
9. Contact information (DPO or privacy contact)
10. Last updated date

## Privacy Policy (CCPA addendum)

Additional required clauses beyond GDPR base:
1. Categories of personal information sold or shared in the last 12 months
2. Right to know (access)
3. Right to delete
4. Right to correct
5. Right to opt out of sale/sharing
6. Non-discrimination statement
7. How to submit requests (with response timeline: 45 days)

## Terms of Service

Required inputs before generation:
- Product name
- Company legal name
- Governing law jurisdiction
- Effective date
- Contact email for legal notices

Required clauses:
1. Acceptance of terms
2. Description of service
3. User eligibility
4. User obligations and prohibited uses
5. Intellectual property ownership
6. Payment terms (if subscription product)
7. Termination conditions
8. Warranty disclaimer
9. Limitation of liability
10. Governing law and dispute resolution
11. Changes to terms

## GDPR Data Processing Agreement

Required inputs:
- Data controller identity
- Data processor identity
- Contact email
- Sub-processors list (if any)
- Data categories and subjects
- Processing purposes

Required clauses: instructions for processing, confidentiality, security measures, sub-processor obligations, data subject rights assistance, deletion/return on termination, audit rights.

## Disclaimer

Required inputs:
- What the content is (e.g., "this document," "this tool's output," "this analysis")
- What it is not (legal advice, medical advice, financial advice — as applicable)
- Contact for professional advice

Minimum content: nature of the material, what it does not constitute, recommendation to seek qualified professional advice.
