# Execution Planning

Channel selection, campaign structure, and success metrics by budget tier.

## Budget tiers

| Tier | Definition | Channel budget range |
|---|---|---|
| bootstrap | Self-funded, < $10K/year marketing spend | $0–$833/month |
| seed | Seed-funded, $10K–$100K/year | $833–$8,333/month |
| growth | Series A+, $100K–$1M/year | $8,333–$83,333/month |
| scale | Series B+, $1M+/year | $83,333+/month |

## Channel selection by budget tier

### Bootstrap

Prioritize organic and owned channels — no paid media budget assumed.

Recommended: content marketing (SEO), community participation (Discord, Reddit, Hacker News, relevant Slack communities), founder personal brand (Twitter/X, LinkedIn), open-source contribution if applicable, cold email to ICP (manual, not automated).

Avoid: paid search, paid social, conferences — too expensive per lead at this tier.

### Seed

Add: limited paid search (target bottom-of-funnel, high-intent keywords only), outbound sales (SDR or founder-led), product-led growth if the product has a free tier, partnership / co-marketing with 1–2 complementary tools.

### Growth

Add: paid social (LinkedIn for B2B, Meta for B2C), broader paid search, demand gen (webinars, content syndication), affiliate or referral program, event participation (not sponsorship — focus on speaking slots).

### Scale

Full-channel coverage. Add: brand awareness campaigns, major conference sponsorships, analyst relations (Gartner, Forrester), category-defining content programs.

## Campaign structure

A campaign has three phases. Messages are assigned per phase, not per channel.

| Phase | Goal | Message emphasis | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | ICP learns the product exists | Problem recognition, category education | 2–4 weeks |
| Consideration | ICP evaluates the product | Differentiation, proof points, social proof | 2–6 weeks |
| Conversion | ICP makes a decision | Risk reduction, trial/demo CTA, pricing clarity | Ongoing |

## Message assignment per phase

- Awareness: primary message (the problem + category)
- Consideration: supporting points (differentiation + proof)
- Conversion: proof elements (specific metrics, customer names, guarantees)

Do not lead with pricing in Awareness. Do not lead with the problem in Conversion.

## Success metrics

Declare metrics per phase. Tie to available data sources.

| Timeframe | Awareness metrics | Consideration metrics | Conversion metrics |
|---|---|---|---|
| 30 days | Impressions, organic traffic | Demo/trial requests, content engagement | Trial-to-paid conversion rate |
| 60 days | Brand search volume trend | Pipeline value, MQL count | CAC (customer acquisition cost) |
| 90 days | Inbound vs outbound ratio | Sales cycle length | Revenue, NPS (first cohort) |

Define what "traction" means before launch. Without a declared target, there is no way to evaluate whether the execution plan worked.
