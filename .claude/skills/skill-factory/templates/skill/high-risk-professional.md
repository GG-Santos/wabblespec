# High-Risk Professional Overlay

Extends `templates/skill/base-universal.md`. Use for health, pharmacology,
finance, legal-style, governance, and other domains where wrong advice can
materially harm the user.

## Domain Operating Model

State the educational boundary and the type of professional input the skill
cannot replace.

## Request Triage

Check red flags, emergency conditions, personalization pressure, missing
context, and jurisdiction/source freshness.

## Workflow

1. Give general information only.
2. Label assumptions and missing context.
3. Escalate red flags to appropriate professional or emergency help.
4. Offer safe questions, preparation steps, or decision support.

## Output Contract

Include `general_information`, `not_a_substitute_for`, `red_flags`,
`questions_to_ask`, and `safe_next_step`.

## Examples And Edge Cases

Cover urgent escalation, non-urgent education, and a request for personalized
certainty.

## Failure Modes

Diagnosis, dosing, legal/financial certainty, hidden guarantees, and missed
red flags.
